What to do with this

Score band is the one that settles whether apply_threshold is right. If 3.75–4.0 advances at a similar rate to 4.5+, the bar is too high and you are discarding real chances.
ATS coverage tests whether the tailoring is worth its LLM calls. If 85% converts like 100%, stop paying for the last 15 points.
Level band tests the stretch strategy — if stretch never advances, drop max_level_number to 1 and save the effort.
Source tells you where to spend sourcing work, and which boards are reposting stale listings.
